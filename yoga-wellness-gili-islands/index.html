Yoga & wellness on the Gili Islands

Compare yoga and wellness on Gili Air, Gili Trawangan and Gili Meno. Start with the island where you are staying, then choose a studio class, a private appointment or a retreat venue.

Gili Air: studios and private sessions

Flowers & Fire has two yoga studios; H2O combines yoga and meditation with massage and accommodation. Slow describes personalised yoga or Pilates and spa treatments, with sessions arranged directly.

Gili Trawangan: garden or beach

Gili Yoga publishes daily class information in its garden-studio setting. Sunset Beach Yoga offers Morning Flow and FlyHigh Yoga by the beach; ask the team for the current times.

Gili Meno: enquire before planning

maoMeno describes yoga and retreat hosting, but its linked schedule is undated. Seri lists massage and retreat space; a current public daily-yoga programme has not been verified.

Planning your visit

Which Gili island has yoga studios listed on Holistic Tribe?

Gili Air has Flowers & Fire and H2O, with private sessions also described by Slow. Gili Trawangan has Gili Yoga and Sunset Beach Yoga. For Gili Meno, contact maoMeno to check which classes are running.

Can I go to either Flowers & Fire studio for a class?

Yoga Garden and Seaside Yoga Studio are different locations on Gili Air. Check the class location first; the profile has a separate map link for each studio.

Is yoga on Gili Meno available as a daily drop-in?

This is not confirmed in the reviewed information. Contact maoMeno about current classes. Seri’s earlier daily-yoga dates have ended, so it is listed for massage and retreat space rather than a current public class timetable.

Where can I find a massage on the Gili Islands?

H2O and Slow list massage on Gili Air. Seri Resort lists massage on Gili Meno. Confirm the treatment, appointment and visitor access directly.

How do I book a yoga class or wellness session?

Open the provider profile and use its website, booking link, WhatsApp or email. Holistic Tribe helps you compare the options; bookings are arranged directly with the provider.
