Yoga retreats in Lombok & the Gili Islands

Explore yoga retreat programmes, retreat venues and stay-based experiences. A retreat host, a scheduled retreat and a resort with wellness facilities are different options; use the profile notes to understand what is listed.

A retreat with published dates

Xanadu and Mana have upcoming programmes listed in the agenda. Follow the organiser’s link to confirm places and the package you want.

Hosting your own retreat

Some listed venues describe space for visiting facilitators or retreat groups. Ask about the shala, accommodation and what is included; a venue listing is not a scheduled retreat.

Check the right property

Xanadu has two locations. Puri Mas’s spa is separate from its beach resort, and the Gili Islands require their own travel plans. Confirm the exact venue before booking.
