Massage in Lombok & the Gili Islands

Compare massage and bodywork at day spas, resorts and wellness spaces. Location matters: the Kuta area, Senggigi, Selong Belanak and the Gili Islands have separate listings and access arrangements.

Day-spa options in Kuta

My Spa and GLAZE publish massage and bodywork information. Use the treatment menu and contact links to choose an appointment.

A resort spa visit

Puri Mas welcomes outside spa guests by appointment, but its wellness location is separate from the beachfront resort. At Kuara, SENJA and Seri, ask about visitor access.

Massage on the islands

H2O and Slow list massage on Gili Air. Seri lists resort massage on Gili Meno. Confirm the treatment and place directly before going.

Planning your visit

Can I visit a resort spa without staying there?

This depends on the venue. Puri Mas explicitly describes outside spa visitors by appointment. For other resorts, ask the team before travelling.

Does every yoga studio also offer massage?

No. This page includes only providers with massage in the reviewed listing. Cocoon’s paused physical treatments are excluded from the massage results.
