Choose your island

Gili Air and Gili Trawangan have several listed yoga spaces. Gili Meno has fewer options, so contact maoMeno before planning around a class.

Planning your visit

Where can I find a yoga class in Lombok?

Start with the studios listed in Kuta, or choose Gili Air or Gili Trawangan if you are staying on those islands. Open a profile to see its location and direct booking links.

Can I book a single class instead of a retreat?

Several studios publish individual classes. Retreat and accommodation listings can work differently; check the availability note on the profile before booking.
