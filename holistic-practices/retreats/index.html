01 / The essentials

A little understanding.
A better first step.

A retreat is time set aside for a programme or personal focus away from everyday routines. Some have fixed dates and a teacher-led group. Others are accommodation packages with optional classes. A venue that hosts retreats may have no public programme you can currently join.

A useful distinction

Read what the package leaves out

Transfers, single-room supplements, treatments and equipment can change the real cost. Compare total package details rather than headline prices.

A useful distinction

The exact place matters

Kuta, Selong Belanak and the Gili Islands involve different travel plans. Confirm the specific property, arrival arrangements and realistic transfer time with the organiser.

Scheduled group retreat

A programme with published dates, an organiser and shared activities.

02 / Stay-based+

Flexible yoga or wellness stay

A stay package built around accommodation and selected activities.

Read more ↗

Arrival dates may be flexible, while classes can follow a public studio schedule. It may feel different from a closed retreat group.

Before you book

Check exactly which classes, meals and treatments are included.

03 / Learning+

Immersion & teacher training

A programme centred on sustained practice or study.

Read more ↗

An immersion and a teacher-training qualification are not interchangeable. Course hours, assessment and recognition need their own checks.

Before you book

Ask whether the programme is recreational study or a professional course.

03 / A little context

Know the words.
Understand the setting.

Where the practice comes from

The word retreat is used for a wide range of contemporary travel and practice programmes. Traditional religious retreats, modern yoga holidays and resort wellness stays can have very different expectations. Ask how the organiser defines the programme and its aims.

Immersion+

A period of focused practice or study; the label does not imply a qualification.

Single supplement+

An additional charge for using a room alone when a package is priced for sharing.

Retreat venue+

A place available to host a programme, distinct from the organiser or teacher.

Inclusions+

The accommodation, food, sessions and services covered by the package price.

04 / From reading to trying

Make your first visit your own.

Three questions to ask

  1. Who leads the programme, and what experience or qualifications do they have?
  2. What is included, what is optional, and what are the cancellation terms?
  3. Which property is used, and how are transfers arranged?

You can ask these questions before booking. Clear answers make it easier to choose a session that fits your plans.

Care & expectations

Good to know

Assess each activity separately, especially intensive breathing, prolonged silence, fasting or demanding physical sessions. Tell the organiser about relevant access and health needs before booking. A retreat is not a substitute for medical or psychological treatment.

Educational information. No guaranteed outcomes or substitute for professional care.

07 / A few final questions

Before you go.

Does a retreat venue always have a retreat I can join?+

No. A venue may host private groups or offer space to organisers. Look for a dated programme with a named host.

Can I join a single class instead?+

Some venues have public classes, while others centre on stays. Use the yoga guide and provider access notes.

Does a date in the agenda mean places are available?+

No. Dates reflect the published programme. Confirm capacity, price and terms directly with the organiser.
