Yoga on Gili Air

Explore yoga on Gili Air through three different settings: Flowers & Fire’s two studios, H2O’s yoga and meditation centre, and the more appointment-led approach at Slow.

Compare 3 providers ↓Classes & events ↗

Two Flowers & Fire studios

The Yoga Garden and Seaside Yoga Studio are part of one provider. Use the separate map links on the profile to reach the correct studio.

A yoga and meditation base

H2O combines yoga and meditation with other wellness activities and accommodation. Ask the team about the sessions running during your stay.

A personal appointment

Slow describes personalised yoga or Pilates and spa treatments. Confirm a session with the team instead of assuming a public group timetable.
