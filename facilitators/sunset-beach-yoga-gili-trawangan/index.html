The place

Yoga with a sea breeze.

Sunset Beach Yoga is a beachside yoga space on Gili Trawangan, with an open-air pavilion and views towards the ocean. Morning Flow and FlyHigh Yoga are part of its offering.

Come for a little movement during your island stay, or get in touch to ask which class would suit you. The team’s WhatsApp and Instagram are the best places to check the latest schedule before visiting.
