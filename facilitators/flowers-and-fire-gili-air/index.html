About

About Flowers & Fire

Flowers & Fire is a wellness and community space on Gili Air, offering daily yoga classes across two unique island studios: the lush, open-air Yoga Garden and the oceanfront Seaside Yoga Studio. More than just a place to practise yoga, Flowers & Fire brings together movement, mindfulness, connection, nourishing food, workshops and island living in a welcoming environment for travellers, residents and retreat guests alike.

✦
Two unique studios
Practise in a bamboo treehouse surrounded by tropical gardens or beside the ocean with expansive sea views.
✦
Yoga for every level
From complete beginners to experienced practitioners, classes are designed to be accessible, supportive and inclusive.
✦
Workshops & community
Sound healing, breathwork, cacao ceremonies and community gatherings create opportunities to explore beyond the yoga mat.
