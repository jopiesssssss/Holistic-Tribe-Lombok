About

Meet Ola Ola Lombok

Ola Ola combines a small surf house with yoga and mat Pilates in Selong Belanak. Its published programme includes Morning Flow, Spicy Vinyasa and Yin Yang Yoga. Classes are described as open to different experience levels, with individual classes and passes available.
