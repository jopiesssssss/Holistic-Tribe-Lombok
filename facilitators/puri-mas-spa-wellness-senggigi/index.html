About the provider

Meet Puri Mas Spa & Wellness Center

Puri Mas offers spa treatments and yoga in its Joglo House or gardens. Its own website explicitly welcomes outside spa visitors by appointment and describes yoga access for non-resident guests. The spa and wellness location is separate from the beachfront resort.
