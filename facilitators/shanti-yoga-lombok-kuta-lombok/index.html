Meet Shanti Holistic Wellness Lombok

Shanti Holistic Wellness Lombok offers private yoga, Reiki, sound healing and cacao experiences. Following its move from a resort studio, the practice works at private spaces and selected venues in Lombok. There is no fixed public studio schedule.
