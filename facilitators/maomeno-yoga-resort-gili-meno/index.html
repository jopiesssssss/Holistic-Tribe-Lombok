Meet maoMeno Yoga Resort

maoMeno is a yoga resort on Gili Meno. Its own website links a yoga programme, retreat-hosting options and a schedule describing Hatha and Flow practice, with private classes by enquiry. Contact the team to check which sessions are running during your visit.
